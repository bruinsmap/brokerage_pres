All threshold-specific estimates remain positive, and the confidence intervals are tight enough to rule out a pooled null effect.

The pooled effect is not huge, but it is too stable across thresholds and specifications to dismiss as noise.

\vspace{0.1cm}
\begin{eqbox}
Across thresholds, one standard-deviation more brokerage raises the odds of atypical patenting by roughly \textbf{5--6\%}. That is modest, but persistent at European scale.
\end{eqbox}
