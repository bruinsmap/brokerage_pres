\small

\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\column{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{softbox}[title=What stands out]
The pooled effect hides a lot of time variation: the 1990s are stronger, the mid-2000s weaken sharply, and later periods recover only partially.
\end{softbox}

\column{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{softbox}[title=Why this matters]
This is not a ``brokerage always wins'' story. It is a context-sensitive mechanism whose value changes with historical conditions.
\end{softbox}
\end{columns}

\vspace{0.1cm}
\begin{eqbox}
The effect is positive in most periods, but the mid-2000s dip shows that brokerage is not a timeless superstar variable --- its payoff rises and falls with historical context.
\end{eqbox}
