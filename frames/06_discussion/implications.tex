\begin{frame}{The Vanishing Premium: Era matters}

\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\column{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{softbox}[title=Temporal Attenuation]
The brokerage advantage is not a "fixed property" of networks; it has declined by ~64\% since the late 1980s.
\end{softbox}

\column{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{softbox}[title=The Connectivity Paradox]
Brokerage returns are strongest during eras of rapid technological expansion and cross-domain recombination (the 1990s).
\end{softbox}
\end{columns}

\vspace{0.3cm}
\begin{accentbox}[title=Limitation]
Patent-based evidence only; informal collaboration, non-patented innovation, and hidden organizational learning remain outside the frame.
\end{accentbox}
\end{frame}
