\begin{frame}{Why this matters beyond one patent dataset}
\small
\begin{softbox}[title=For theory]
Brokerage is linked to a qualitatively different kind of inventive output, not only to more output.
\end{softbox}

\vspace{0.15cm}
\begin{softbox}[title=For firms and places]
If breakthrough recombination matters, firms should protect bridging roles across teams and applicants, while regions need collaborative openness plus enough related variety to make distant recombination feasible.
\end{softbox}

\vspace{0.15cm}
\begin{accentbox}[title=Caveat]
This is patent-based evidence, so informal collaboration, non-patented innovation, and hidden organizational learning remain outside the frame.
\end{accentbox}
\end{frame}
