\begin{frame}{Conclusion}

\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\column{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{accentbox}[title=Core finding]
Inventor brokerage \pos{positively} associated with atypical recombination -- robust across thresholds, network definitions, and time periods.
\end{accentbox}

\column{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{statbox}[title=Policy implication]
Innovation policy should target \textbf{who bridges whom}, not only the volume of R\&D. 
But in a context-sensitive waywit attention to underlying diversity of the innovation environment.
\end{statbox}
\end{columns}

\vspace{0.3cm}

\takeaway{Cultivating bridges is effective, but its value depends on the surrounding innovation environment's diversity.}
\end{frame}
