\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\column{0.49\textwidth}
\begin{block}{What makes the panel persuasive?}
It follows inventors through changing collaboration neighborhoods instead of collapsing everything to regions or firms.
\end{block}

\column{0.49\textwidth}
\begin{block}{What is the main limitation?}
We observe formal patent collaboration, not every informal knowledge flow, so the estimates likely understate broader brokerage effects.
\end{block}
