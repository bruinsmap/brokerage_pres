\begin{frame}{Data architecture}
\small
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\column{0.59\textwidth}
\begin{softbox}[title=What goes into the panel?]
\begin{tabular}{p{0.52\linewidth}p{0.38\linewidth}}
Patent applications with inventor and CPC information & EPO PATSTAT, April 2024 \\
EU-28 inventors assigned to NUTS2 regions & 1984--2023 \\
Network sample: patents with at least two inventors & 5,452,904 inventor-window observations \\
Estimation sample: patents with at least two CPC codes & 2,459,067 inventor-window observations \\
\end{tabular}
\end{softbox}

\vspace{0.2cm}
\begin{accentbox}[title=Why five-year windows?]
They are long enough to estimate collaboration structure reliably, but still short enough to let network positions and inventive outcomes move over time.
\end{accentbox}

\column{0.37\textwidth}
\begin{statbox}[title=What the sample buys us]
\textbf{Scale:} Europe-wide coverage\\[0.12cm]
\textbf{Unit:} inventor-window observations\\[0.12cm]
\textbf{Advantage:} we can compare inventors within the same historical context rather than only across regions.
\end{statbox}

\vspace{0.22cm}
\begin{softbox}[title=Interpretation note]
The smaller final window partly reflects publication lag, not an abrupt collapse in inventive activity.
\end{softbox}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}
