\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\column{0.49\textwidth}
\begin{softbox}[title=Brokerage]
Bridges expose inventors to non-redundant knowledge.\\[0.15cm]
Main measure: standardized brokerage / constraint-based network position.\\[0.15cm]
Mean $=0.203$, median $=0.082$.
\end{softbox}

\column{0.49\textwidth}
\begin{softbox}[title=Atypicality]
Patents are atypical when CPC subclass pairs occur much less often than expected historically.\\[0.15cm]
At $Z<0$, 17.5\% of inventor-window observations contain at least one atypical patent; at $Z<-2$, 14.6\% do.
\end{softbox}
\end{columns}

\vspace{0.3cm}

\begin{eqbox}
\[
Z_{ij}=\frac{O_{ij}-E_{ij}}{\sigma_{ij}}
\]
Low $Z$ means the pairing is rarer than chance.
\end{eqbox}
