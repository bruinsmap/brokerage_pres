\begin{frame}{How we measure the mechanism}
\scriptsize
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\column{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{softbox}[title=Brokerage]
We proxy brokerage with a constraint-based network position: low constraint means the inventor connects neighborhoods that are not already densely tied to one another.\\[0.08cm]
\textbf{Distribution:} mean $=0.203$, median $=0.082$.
\end{softbox}

\column{0.48\textwidth}
\begin{softbox}[title=Atypicality]
Patents are atypical when CPC subclass pairs appear together much less often than expected from historical co-occurrence.\\[0.08cm]
\textbf{Incidence:} 17.5\% of inventor-window observations contain at least one atypical patent at $Z<0$, and 14.6\% do at $Z<-2$.
\end{softbox}
\end{columns}

\vspace{0.1cm}
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\column{0.54\textwidth}
\begin{eqbox}
\[
Z_{ij}=\frac{O_{ij}-E_{ij}}{\sigma_{ij}}
\]
Lower $Z$ means the pairing is rarer than chance.
\end{eqbox}

\column{0.42\textwidth}
\begin{accentbox}[title=Thresholds used in the talk]
$Z<0$ for broad novelty, $Z<-2$ as the preferred benchmark, and $Z<-5$ / $Z<-14$ for rarer combinations.
\end{accentbox}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}
