\begin{frame}{Test beyond the baseline}
\scriptsize
\begin{columns}[T,onlytextwidth]
\column{0.31\textwidth}
\begin{softbox}[title=Baseline]
Does higher brokerage increase the count of atypical patents an inventor produces?
\end{softbox}

\column{0.31\textwidth}
\begin{softbox}[title=Network variants]
Does the signal survive if we isolate interfirm ties or downweight within-firm ties?
\end{softbox}

\column{0.34\textwidth}
\begin{softbox}[title=Historical contingency]
Is brokerage a permanent advantage, or does its payoff depend on the period?
\end{softbox}
\end{columns}

\vspace{0.18cm}
\begin{accentbox}[title=Why these extensions matter]
If the relationship is real, it should hold beyond one network definition. If the mechanism is substantive, it should also tell us \textbf{which boundaries matter most} and \textbf{when brokerage pays off}.
\end{accentbox}
\end{frame}
